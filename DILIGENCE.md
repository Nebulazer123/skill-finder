# Diligence Statement

In creating Skill Finder, I collaborated with Codex to plan, draft, test, and refine the skill instructions, examples, and validation checks.

AI assistance contributed to brainstorming the workflow, structuring the search and ranking rules, drafting public-facing documentation, and running local validation. I reviewed and directed the work, made the final decisions about what to include, and take responsibility for the published content.

This repository is intentionally limited to public-safe project materials. Private course notes, internal validation packets, local paths, credentials, and personal workspace files were excluded from the public package.

Skill Finder is provided as a workflow aid. Users should review candidate tools, install commands, licenses, and safety boundaries before taking action.
